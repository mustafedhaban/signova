# Signova Design

## Scene

Bright office, laptop open before a client call. User needs a trustworthy signature in seconds.

## Theme

Light default. Dark available. Restrained palette.

## Colors (OKLCH)

| Token | Role |
|-------|------|
| Primary | Deep slate navy, trust |
| Accent | Sky blue, actions and links |
| Background | Warm slate-50, not pure white |
| Destructive | Red, delete only |

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans for UI. Tight tracking on headings. Body max 65ch.

## Motion

200ms ease-out on color and opacity. Active press on buttons. No layout-shifting hovers.

## Banned

Purple gradients, glass cards, hero metric rows, emoji icons, side-stripe borders.
