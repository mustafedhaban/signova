# Signova

## Product Purpose

Signova helps teams create, manage, and deploy professional email signatures without design tools or IT tickets.

## Users

Marketing leads and ops managers at SMBs who need brand-consistent signatures across Gmail, Outlook, and Apple Mail.

## Register

product

## Tone

Clear, confident, calm. No hype. Every screen answers one question.

## Anti-references

- Generic purple-gradient SaaS
- Feature grids with identical icon cards
- Dashboards cluttered with metrics nobody uses
- Copy that says "elevate", "seamless", or "next-gen"

## Strategic principles

1. Signatures first: the preview is the product.
2. Three clicks to copy HTML.
3. Hide power features until needed (teams, orgs).
